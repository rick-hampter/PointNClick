This ReadMe contains the solutions to all puzzles in the game and all of the controls in the game.

Press I to toggle debug mode. Debug mode shows the game's values on the screen in pink.
Debug mode allows for the revisiting of any scene in the game using number keys and the Menu with M.

After pressing Play, use the key under the rock to open the front door.
Upon entering the Hallway, click the coat on the rack to get the first key (the pink key) needed to unlock the door in the basement.
Go through the door in the hallway to enter the Foyer. Go down the stairs to get to the basement and remove the pink lock.
Go up the stairs until you get to the Landing at the top of the stairs and enter the Study on the left.
Turn on the light switch on the left side of the room. Slide the painting over to reveal a safe. 
Enter the Landing. Take the pin off of the globe and use it to pick the lock on the safe.
Take the orange key from behind the Teddy Bear and use it to open the orage lock on the door in the basement.
